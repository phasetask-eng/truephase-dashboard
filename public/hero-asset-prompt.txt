TRUEPHASE — HERO ASSET BRIEF
============================
Status: NOT YET GENERATED. The image tool available in this session was blocked
by a payment issue on the connected account, so no asset was produced and no
credits were spent. The slot in app/page.tsx is an intentional placeholder.

WHY THIS MATTERS
The locked visual style is image-led. Its hero pairs a massive black headline
with a photorealistic 3D render of a physical, tactile object. Substituting a
CSS drawing or a gradient collapses the direction. Generate the real asset.

SLOT
  Aspect ratio : 1:1 (square)
  Position     : right-hand column of the hero, roughly 45% of page width
  Renders at   : up to ~640x640 CSS px, so export 1280x1280 or larger
  Background   : must match the page canvas #e5e5e5 exactly, or be cut out to
                 transparent PNG
  File         : save as public/hero.png, then replace the <figure
                 className="asset-slot"> block in app/page.tsx with next/image

PROMPT
Photorealistic 3D product render, studio product photography style. A single
chunky cube of pale raw concrete with visible aggregate texture and softly
chamfered edges, centred on a seamless flat warm light gray background.
Embedded into the concrete: a smooth matte black telephone handset form
emerging from one face, a strip of warm oak wood grain inlaid along one edge, a
small protruding pale mint green cylinder and a small bright lemon yellow
sphere. Soft neutral studio lighting from the upper left, gentle contact shadow
beneath, no harsh reflections, no glow. Clean, tactile, material, gallery-like
product shot. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no branding
anywhere. Square composition, object centred with generous negative space
around it.

SUGGESTED MODEL
  bytedance-seedream-5-pro  (photorealism and product shots)
  or any photoreal model capable of 2K output

MUST AVOID
  - Any rendered text, letters, numbers, logos or brand marks
  - Dark or gradient backgrounds
  - Neon glow, lens flare, sci-fi treatment
  - Stock-photo people, offices or headsets
  - Saturated colour beyond the two accent notes:
      mint   #d1ffca
      yellow #fff100

ACCENT DISCIPLINE
Mint and yellow appear only as small protruding elements. They are accent-only
tokens in this system and must never become large surface fills.
